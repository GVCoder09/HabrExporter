## [Unreleased]

## [1.0] - 2026-10-18

First public release.

* Save a Habr article with comments and images as PDF, HTML, DOCX, EPUB, FB2, Markdown or JSON.
* Batch export of a user's bookmarks, an author's articles, or a list of links from a file.
* PyQt6 window with preview, command-line mode (`habr-exporter-cli`), Russian and English interface.
